=== FACEBOOK CAPTION ===

A second visit, a new idea, and a tattoo with a very personal story. 🐉

For this piece, the client returned to LanPinkTattoo with an idea for a dragon connected to his zodiac year. But he did not want an overly fierce or aggressive dragon. He wanted something more free-flowing — a dragon moving through mountains and clouds, like a journey of exploring nature and the world.

Through the consultation process, Lan Pink and the client developed the composition together, with the dragon as the main subject, mountains and clouds creating depth, and a blood moon adding a strong visual focal point.

The meaning behind the piece is just as personal: freedom, exploration, and the courage to experience new things, while still being strong enough to protect yourself when life gets difficult.

A tattoo that is not only about how it looks, but also about the story behind the person wearing it.
