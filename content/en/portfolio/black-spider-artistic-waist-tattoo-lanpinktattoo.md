## Why Is Placement Important for a Waist Tattoo?

With a waist tattoo, the same design can create a very different overall impression depending on its size, direction, and length.

Before getting tattooed, it is worth discussing several details with your tattoo artist:

- The desired size of the tattoo.
- The direction in which the design should develop across the waist.
- The level of detail that works well with the available skin area.
- How the tattoo can follow the body's natural curves.
- Whether the design could be expanded or connected with future tattoos.

Lan Pink usually prefers to discuss these details directly with each client before starting the tattoo, helping determine a suitable composition rather than simply applying a ready-made design.
