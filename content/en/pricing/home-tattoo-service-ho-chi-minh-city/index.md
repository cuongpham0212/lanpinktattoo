## Booking deposits, cancellations and rescheduling

To confirm an at-home appointment, the advance payment consists of **two separate amounts with different purposes**:

- **Round-trip travel fee:** An advance payment for Lan's journey to your location and back to the studio.
- **VND 200,000 booking deposit:** A deposit that reserves your appointment time, allowing Lan to prepare the design and equipment and organize the working schedule.

**Total advance payment = Round-trip travel fee + VND 200,000 booking deposit.**

The **VND 200,000 is not an additional service charge**. When the tattoo appointment is completed, this amount is deducted directly from the tattoo price.

**Payment example**

Suppose your tattoo costs VND 1,500,000 and the round-trip travel fee is VND 600,000:

- You pay VND 800,000 in advance: VND 600,000 for travel and VND 200,000 to reserve the appointment.
- After the tattoo is completed, you pay the remaining VND 1,300,000.
- The total cost is VND 2,100,000. Neither the booking deposit nor the travel fee is charged twice.

These figures illustrate the calculation only; they are not fixed travel rates.

**If you cancel before Lan departs**

If you notify Lan that you wish to cancel before the journey begins:

- **100% of the prepaid round-trip travel fee will be refunded**, because the journey has not started.
- **The VND 200,000 booking deposit is non-refundable**, because Lan has reserved the time, made preparations and declined other bookings that would overlap with your appointment.

This booking condition will be clearly communicated and must be accepted before you pay the deposit.

**If you reschedule before Lan departs**

If you request a different date or time before Lan begins traveling, the entire advance payment will **be retained and transferred to the new appointment**. You do not need to pay another VND 200,000 booking deposit.

Lan will check the available schedule so both parties can agree on a new time. If the address or travel conditions change, any difference in travel fees will be reconciled before the new appointment is confirmed.

**Lan confirms the appointment again before departure**

Before traveling, **Lan will proactively send a message or make a phone call** to reconfirm the appointment time, address and your readiness for the tattoo session.

If Lan cannot reach you, the appointment will not automatically be treated as reconfirmed.

You may notify Lan of a cancellation or rescheduling request before departure, and the corresponding policy will apply.

**If you cancel or reschedule after Lan has departed**

Once Lan has started traveling for a confirmed appointment, working time and travel expenses have already been incurred.

If you cancel, reschedule, are absent or decline the appointment after this point, **the VND 200,000 booking deposit is non-refundable**. The prepaid travel amount will be used to cover actual travel expenses incurred. Any remaining balance will be refunded. If additional expenses arise, both parties will discuss and agree on how to handle them.

These conditions must be disclosed and accepted before the deposit is collected and will be applied in accordance with applicable law.

**If you change the appointment address**

You must inform Lan and obtain confirmation before changing the location.

If the new address affects the travel fee, Lan will explain the difference so you can decide before proceeding.

**If you switch to an appointment at the studio**

If you decide to visit the studio **before Lan departs**:

- The prepaid travel fee will be refunded in full.
- The VND 200,000 booking deposit will be retained for your studio appointment and deducted from the tattoo price upon completion.

If Lan has already departed, any travel expenses incurred will be reconciled before both parties agree on the next steps.

**If Lan Pink has to cancel**

If Lan cancels before departure for a reason attributable to the studio, you may choose **a full refund of all advance payments, including the VND 200,000 booking deposit**, or transfer the entire payment to a new appointment.

If the tattoo cannot be performed because the actual conditions are unsafe, both parties will discuss an appropriate solution based on the circumstances, responsibilities and applicable rules.

## Preparing the space and how the appointment works

The tattooing area should be clean, well lit and spacious enough for equipment and suitable seating or lying positions.

For example, a forearm tattoo requires stable arm support; shoulder or back tattoos require sufficient working space; and leg or thigh tattoos may require an appropriate chair or surface to lie on.

An unsuitable working position can cause fatigue and make precise tattooing more difficult. It can also make it harder for you to remain comfortable and still throughout the session.

Lan may discuss furniture and positioning in advance or ask you to send photos of the proposed tattooing area.

Please minimize dust, cigarette smoke, pets and unnecessary foot traffic in the working area. Lan will bring the necessary equipment and supplies and check hygiene and safety conditions before proceeding.

**An at-home setting does not automatically provide the same conditions as a studio.** If the location is unsuitable, Lan may suggest adjustments or recommend completing the tattoo at the studio.

**The booking process has five steps:**

1. **Send your request:** Share your design or idea, approximate size, body placement, address and preferred time.
2. **Consultation and quote:** Lan confirms whether the tattoo is suitable, its price, the travel fee and the deposit conditions.

4. **Reconfirmation before departure:** Lan contacts you to make sure you are ready for the appointment.
5. **Tattoo session and completion:** Lan checks the design and working conditions, performs the tattoo when appropriate, provides aftercare guidance and collects the remaining balance.

**If you want to change the design after Lan arrives**

Lan can discuss changes to the design, size or placement. If the changes increase the price, Lan will explain the new amount and proceed only with your agreement.

If you do not accept the revised price, the original design may still be completed if it remains suitable and safe.

**Tattoo aftercare**
